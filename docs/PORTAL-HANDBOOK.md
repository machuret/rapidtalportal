# The RapidTal Portal — A Friendly Handbook

*What the portal does for you, what it does for your VA, and how the two of you work together inside it.*

---

## The big idea

The RapidTal Portal is the shared home where a **business owner (the Client)** and their **virtual assistant (the VA)** work together. Think of it as one calm place that does three things:

1. **Hands work off cleanly** — you ask, your VA does, you approve. No long email chains.
2. **Keeps everything visible** — you always know what's happening, without having to chase.
3. **Gets smarter over time** — the more your business "teaches" the portal about itself, the sharper your VA *and* the built-in AI become.

Behind the scenes, **RapidTal** (the agency) runs the business side and is there to help if anything goes wrong — but the day-to-day is just you and your VA.

There are three kinds of people in the portal:

| Who | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Client** | The business owner / manager. Requests work, approves it, sees how things are going. |
| **VA** | The virtual assistant. Does the work, logs their day, uses the AI tools to work faster. |
| **RapidTal** | The agency, working quietly in the background — placements, support, and mediation if needed. |

---

## What the Portal offers the **Client**

Everything a Client sees is grouped around the jobs they actually care about: directing work, staying in the loop, and getting more value from their AI.

### 🏠 Your home base
- **Dashboard** — your 30-second morning check-in. It shows what your VA delivered, what's waiting on your approval, anything overdue or due soon, and a **"Get the most from RapidTal" setup panel** that tells you exactly what to fill in to get more value (starting with your Company DNA).

### 🤝 Directing the work
- **Tasks** — a shared to-do board you and your VA both see live. You **Request work**, it appears on the board, and you watch it move across columns as it gets done. When work is ready, you **Approve** it or **Request changes** with a note — right from the board or the dashboard.
- **My Team** — meet your VA(s), see their details, and approve or decline any **time-off requests** they make.
- **Supervision** — a calm overview of how your team is doing: what they've delivered, hours, daily logs, and gentle flags if anything is overdue or someone's gone quiet.
- **Reports** — a clean, shareable **monthly report** of what was delivered, with month-over-month trends. Print it, save as PDF, or copy it into an email.

### 🧠 Your company's AI brain
- **Company DNA** — the single most important page. It's your business's core profile (who you are, what you offer, your voice, your rules). **Everything the AI does reads from here**, so the more complete it is, the smarter and more on-brand everything becomes. The page shows a completeness meter so you always know what's left.
- **Vault** — your company's knowledge library. Drop in documents, web pages, or pasted text and they become searchable knowledge the AI can use.
- **Ask the Vault** — ask any question in plain English and get an answer built only from *your* company's own materials, with links to where each fact came from.
- **Company Brain** — a single home showing how capable your AI has become (a "Brain Score" that climbs as you feed it), a plain-English journal of what it's learned, and a **coverage view** of what it knows and where the gaps are.
- **Brain Analytics** — what your team is asking the AI, how often it has a good answer, and which questions to close gaps on.

### 🧰 Day-to-day tools
- **CRM** — track your contacts and leads through simple stages, with notes and a running history.
- **Access** — a secure, encrypted vault for the logins your VA needs. Passwords are encrypted, every reveal is logged, and nobody has to share credentials over chat.
- **Notebook** — a private shared workspace for you and your VA (more on this below).
- **Messages** — your shared chat with your VA.

### 📘 Always-on help
- A **Guide** button is on every page, and every section has a short "what is this?" explainer that links to the full guide. You're never more than one tap from help.

## What the Portal offers the **VA**

The VA's side is built around doing great work, working faster with AI, and keeping the relationship clear.

### 🏠 Their workspace
- **Dashboard** — a quick read on the day: open tasks, what's due today, anything overdue, and whether they've logged their day yet.
- **Tasks** — the same shared board the client sees. The VA picks up work, moves it along, and marks it **ready for review** when it's done.
- **Daily Log** — a two-minute end-of-day summary (what they did, wins, blockers, tomorrow's plan). The client reads these, so it's how a VA's work stays visible and trust gets built.

### 🤖 Working faster with AI
- **Ask the Vault** — instant answers from the client's own documents (no more digging through files).
- **Compose** — writes emails, replies, and posts in the client's voice, grounded in the Vault so the details are right.
- **Content** — a studio for longer marketing drafts (blogs, posts, emails), which the client approves before anything goes out.
- **Tools** — a growing kit of AI helpers for everyday client work (SEO metadata, Google Business posts, content briefs, hashtags, follow-ups, and more) — each one already grounded in the client's brand, so the output is ready to use, not generic.
- **SOPs** — step-by-step playbooks for recurring work. A VA can follow one in a guided runner so it's done the right way every time.

### 🧾 Their "employment" home
- **My Job** — everything about the working relationship in one place: their contract terms, **time-off requests** and remaining balance, attendance, and self-generated **documents** (certificate of employment, invoices, tax summary) built from their logged days and rate.
- **Issues** — if something needs RapidTal's help, a VA can raise it here for mediation.

### 🧠 Shared knowledge (read access)
- **Company DNA**, **Vault**, **Company Report** — VAs can read the company's brain so their work is always on-brand and informed.

## How the **Client ↔ VA relationship** works in the portal

The whole portal is built around a few simple, repeating loops.

### 1. The work loop — request → do → approve
This is the heart of it:

1. **The Client requests work** ("Draft the June newsletter") — it lands on the shared **Tasks** board and the VA is notified (in-app and by email).
2. **The VA does the work**, moving the card across the board so progress is always visible, and marks it **Ready for review** when done.
3. **The Client is notified**, opens it, and either **Approves** it (✅ done — the VA is told) or **Requests changes** with a note (it goes back to the VA with the feedback attached).

No status-update emails, nothing falling through the cracks — the board *is* the single source of truth.

### 2. The visibility loop — daily logs & supervision
- At the end of each day, the **VA writes a quick Daily Log**.
- The **Client reads it** (on the dashboard, in My Team, or in Supervision) and can leave feedback.
- **Supervision** rolls this up so the Client can see momentum at a glance and gently notice if anything's slipping — without micromanaging.

### 3. The conversation loop — Messages & the Notebook
- **Messages** is the shared chat for quick back-and-forth, so work talk doesn't get lost in personal inboxes.
- The **Notebook** is the shared, longer-form workspace — SOPs, account registers, content calendars, weekly reports, meeting notes. Both people can read and edit every page, and it comes pre-loaded with handy templates.
  - 🔒 **Privacy promise:** the Notebook is private to the Client and VA. **RapidTal admins cannot read what's written there** — they can only see that activity is happening, never the content.

### 4. The smarter-over-time loop — the shared brain
This is what makes the portal more than a to-do list:

- The **Client fills in Company DNA** and adds documents to the **Vault**.
- That knowledge powers **every** AI feature the VA uses — Ask the Vault, Compose, the Tools, content drafting.
- As the team uses the AI and gives a quick 👍 / 👎, the portal **distils what it learns** and gets sharper — the Client can watch this in the **Company Brain** score and journal.
- The result: the more the Client invests in the brain, the more effective the VA becomes. It's a flywheel.

### 5. The people loop — time off & getting help
- When a VA needs **time off**, they request it in **My Job**; the Client gets notified and **approves or declines** it in My Team. The VA's leave balance updates automatically.
- If something needs a neutral hand, a VA can **raise an issue** to RapidTal for mediation — keeping the working relationship healthy.

## What's shared, and what's private

| Space | Client sees | VA sees | RapidTal sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tasks board | ✅ | ✅ | (admin, when supporting) |
| Messages | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| **Notebook** | ✅ | ✅ | **❌ content is private** |
| Daily Logs | ✅ | ✅ (own) | (admin) |
| Company DNA / Vault | ✅ edit | ✅ read | (admin) |
| Access (passwords) | ✅ | ✅ (scoped) | encrypted — never plain text |

## A typical rhythm

- **Morning (Client):** open the Dashboard, glance at what's awaiting approval and anything overdue, request any new work.
- **During the day (VA):** pick up tasks, use Ask-the-Vault / Compose / Tools to move fast, mark work ready for review.
- **End of day (VA):** write the Daily Log.
- **Anytime (Client):** approve delivered work, read logs, message the VA, top up the Company DNA / Vault to make everything smarter.
- **Monthly (Client):** open Reports, see the value delivered, share it.
